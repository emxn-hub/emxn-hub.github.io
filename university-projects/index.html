PULSE-UP!

PULSE-UP! — POWER YOUR POTENTIAL

Final university project | Sheffield Hallam University | Brand identity, packaging, merchandise and advertising

The brief was to create a startup from scratch. I developed PULSE-UP!, an energy drink brand concept, translating its visual identity into a range of packaging designs, lifestyle product mockups, merchandise and promotional media.

BRAND GUIDELINES

A 35-page visual identity manual documenting the PULSE-UP! brand system, from responsive logos and typography to packaging and real-world applications.

PULSE-UP! — Full Brand Guidelines (PDF)Download PDF

LOGO SYSTEM

Main and reversed wordmarks, landscape and portrait lockups with the lightning-can icon, and responsive minimum sizes: logo 120px web / 20mm print; icon 40px / 10mm; favicon 20px / 5mm.

TYPOGRAPHY

Ninja Attack — logo. Archivo Black — uppercase headings. Gotham — subheadings.

COLOUR PALETTE

#FFC000

#C1AFD4

#4FA8B6

#C1FF72

Pulsating yellow (#FFC000) is the primary brand colour. The can designs combine intricate geometric patterns with gradient blends.

BRAND APPLICATIONS

The manual demonstrates the identity across business cards, retail apparel, mugs, presentation boxes and in-scene product advertising.

THE PRODUCT RANGE

Five distinct colourways use bold typography and geometric patterns to create a recognisable family of products.
