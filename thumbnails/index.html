Behind the Click

A thumbnail is the first impression of a video. Before a viewer hears a word or sees an edit, the thumbnail and title have to earn their attention.

My approach combines bold colour, clear visual hierarchy, expressive characters and storytelling to create curiosity at a glance. I design for readability on mobile, audience relevance and click-through potential, while making sure the visual promise reflects the content itself.
